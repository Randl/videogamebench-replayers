Warcraft II recorder

Save state pauses briefly and saves the emulator and recording together. Restore state returns to that point without replaying inputs. Discarded inputs and video are archived. These controls are experimental.

Ready. Click Play & record to boot the game.

Click the game to give it keyboard focus. Left-click selects; right-click gives orders; drag to select a group. Keyboard shortcuts and arrow keys work while the game has focus. Inputs use the benchmark’s mouse movement and key-press behavior, so clicks take a little time.

Save recording writes the recorded inputs without closing the game. It does not save emulator state. The video is finalized by Stop & save. Continue saved recording replays the saved inputs at their original timing, then lets you extend the same recording. Real-time play continues when you switch tabs. Restart starts a new game and archives the previous recording.
